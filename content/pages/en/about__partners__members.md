title: members
---

The Paris Institute for Advanced Studies maintains close ties with 13 research institutions in the Île-de-France region, all of which are members of the Institute.These institutions share the Paris IAS’s commitment to interdisciplinary scientific excellence and contribute actively to its programmes. Their involvement ensures that the activities carried out at the Hôtel de Lauzun reach a wider audience and strengthens the regional academic community. Each year, the Paris IAS hosts scientific events organised by its members at its premises, located at the Hôtel de Lauzun on the Île Saint-Louis, thus offering an exceptional historic setting conducive to dialogue and academic collaboration.
