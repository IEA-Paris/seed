_Philanthropy & Patronage — Paris Institute for Advanced Study_

The Paris Institute for Advanced Study is a non-profit organisation dedicated to fostering the highest level of independent, interdisciplinary research. Every contribution — from individuals, foundations, and corporations — directly enables the free pursuit of ideas that matter.

---

## Why Support Paris IAS?

Advanced research does not happen by accident. It requires time, freedom, and the right environment. Paris IAS provides exactly that: a singular place where exceptional scholars from across the world are given the space, resources, and intellectual community to ask the questions that others cannot.

Your support sustains:

- **Residential fellowships** for outstanding researchers from over 64 countries
- **Public events** — lectures, seminars, and symposia open to all
- **Interdisciplinary programmes** bridging the humanities, social sciences, and natural sciences
- **Knowledge dissemination** through publications, films, and podcasts freely accessible worldwide
- The **internationalization of French research** and its global influence

Since its founding in 2008, Paris IAS has welcomed more than **500 researchers** from **64+ countries**, partnered with **300+ academic institutions**, and produced a programme of over **100 annual events** open to the public.

## Individual Giving

Individuals who share our commitment to free inquiry and the advancement of knowledge are invited to support Paris IAS through a direct donation.

Donations fund new fellowships, expand our public programme, and help us welcome the most talented researchers — regardless of their country of origin or discipline.

**Tax benefits for individual donors (France):**

| Tax type | Deduction rate | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Income tax (*impôt sur le revenu*) | **66%** of donation | 20% of taxable income |
| Wealth tax (*IFI*) | **75%** of donation | €50,000 per year |

*Donors receive a fiscal receipt within one month of their contribution.*

## Corporate & Institutional Partnership

Paris IAS welcomes partnerships with companies, foundations, and institutions that wish to associate their name with excellence in independent research.

Corporate patronage offers a unique opportunity to:

- Support cutting-edge, international research with measurable societal impact
- Gain visibility within a prestigious scientific and cultural network
- Engage your teams and stakeholders with our public programme
- Demonstrate a genuine commitment to knowledge, openness, and the long term

**Tax benefit for corporate donors (France):**
Companies may deduct **60% of their donation** from corporate tax (*impôt sur les sociétés*), capped at 0.5% of pre-tax revenue (*chiffre d'affaires HT*).

## Foundations & Legacy Giving

Paris IAS accepts gifts from private foundations and welcomes discussions around legacy giving and planned philanthropy. For institutions wishing to establish a named fellowship or fund a dedicated research programme, we are happy to explore tailored partnership arrangements.
