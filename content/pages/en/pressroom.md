## About Paris IAS

The Paris Institute for Advanced Study (Paris IAS) is an internationally recognised centre for advanced research in the humanities and social sciences. Founded in 2011 and hosted on the historic Île Saint-Louis, Paris IAS welcomes outstanding scholars from around the world to pursue ambitious, interdisciplinary research projects.

### Recent highlights

- Over **500 researchers** from **64+ countries** welcomed since 2008
- More than **300 academic partner institutions** worldwide
- Annual scientific programme of **100+ events** open to the public
- Publications, films, and podcasts freely available at **paris-iea.fr**
