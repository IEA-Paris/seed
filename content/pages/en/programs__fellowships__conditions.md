page: "home"
---

The IEA in Paris guarantees to cover the costs associated with each resident’s stay in France. The amount of this support is adjusted according to the researcher’s personal circumstances and the type of call for applications.

Once an application has been accepted, the Institute contacts each resident to clarify the legal, administrative and financial arrangements for their stay, and to define the contractual terms.

The IEA assists residents with all their administrative procedures, particularly with regard to obtaining a visa. In this regard, the Institute provides the necessary documents and may liaise with the relevant authorities to facilitate the procedures.

Furthermore, the Institute supports the development of residents’ scientific activities by providing financial and logistical support. This support may include the organisation of academic events (symposia, conferences, workshops), the provision of training opportunities, as well as assistance with translation and publication.
