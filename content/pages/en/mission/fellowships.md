---
title: "Welcoming researchers: a long-standing mission"
---

The multidisciplinary hosting of top-level international researchers in residence forms the historical foundation of the IEA’s scientific policy. The Institute supports fundamental research across all fields of the humanities and social sciences with the aim of advancing knowledge and promoting the internationalisation of universities in the Île-de-France region.

Researchers are welcomed in optimal living and working conditions. They have offices at the Hôtel de Lauzun, provided by the City of Paris, an iconic venue that fosters exchange and interdisciplinarity. Their accommodation at the Cité internationale universitaire de Paris is also a strong symbol of the commitment to research and international openness

Research stays at the IEA actively contribute to enriching the local higher education and research ecosystem. In partnership with the leading universities and grandes écoles in Paris and the surrounding region, the Institute welcomes cohorts of international researchers of various nationalities from diverse disciplinary and cultural backgrounds every year.

Freed from the usual academic constraints, these researchers can devote themselves fully to their projects. The environment fosters intellectual freedom and collective intelligence, conducive to the emergence of new research perspectives. The diversity that characterises the Institute nurtures a fruitful dialogue between different scientific and national cultures, which rarely have the opportunity to interact. The juxtaposition of these approaches thus helps to bring forth fresh perspectives and stimulate scientific innovation.
