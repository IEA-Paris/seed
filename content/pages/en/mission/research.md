---
title: "Breaking down barriers in research"
---

The Institute works to foster engagement with local and international research, civil society and socio-economic communities, with a view to increasing the impact of research in the humanities, transcending disciplinary and sectoral boundaries.

Forums bringing together academics, members of the public, economic and civil society experts, as well as policy-makers, encourage reflection on current and future social, societal and environmental challenges.

Regular meetings offer researchers the opportunity to organise events and publish scientific work accessible to a wide audience.
