---
title: Ethics Committee
---
The Ethics Committee of the Paris Institute for Advanced Study (Paris IAS) safeguards the integrity of the research carried out within the Institute. Composed of scholars drawn from a range of disciplines, the Committee reviews the ethical dimensions of Fellows' projects and advises on questions of research conduct, data protection, and academic responsibility.

Its work ensures that the Institute's commitment to free and ambitious inquiry is matched by rigorous ethical standards, fostering an environment of trust in which interdisciplinary research can flourish responsibly.
