---
title: "Networks"
---

The IEA in Paris is part of several key networks at European and international level.
The Network of European Institutes for Advanced Study (NetIAS) brings together many European institutes committed to promoting fundamental research and interdisciplinary collaboration.
At a global level, the Institute is a member of the University-Based Institutes for Advanced Study (UBIAS), which brings together hundreds of university-affiliated institutions and fosters global scientific dialogue.
Furthermore, the IEA actively participates in the French Network of Institutes for Advanced Study (RFIEA), which includes seven institutes for advanced study in France: the Collégium de Lyon, the CUT de Rennes, the IMéRA d’Aix-Marseille, the IEA de Nantes, the Institut MAK’IT de Montpellier, the Studium Loire Valley and the IEA de Paris.
These affiliations enhance the Institute’s international appeal and facilitate researcher mobility.
