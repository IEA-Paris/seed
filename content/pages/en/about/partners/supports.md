---
title: "Support"
---

The Paris Institute for Advanced Studies receives support from several public institutions, private partners, and national and European programmes. Among these supporters are the City of Paris, the Ministry of Higher Education and Research, and the France 2030 programme via the RFIEA+ Laboratory of Excellence. The Institute also receives European funding, notably through the FIAS programme, co-funded by the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions, as well as from major international foundations, including the NOMIS Foundation, which supports the Food Socioscope project.
The Institute also benefits from the support of the Riksbankens Jubileumsfond (RJ) for hosting Swedish researchers under its long-term non-thematic programme.
This diverse range of support ensures the Institute’s scientific independence and the quality of the conditions offered to researchers.
