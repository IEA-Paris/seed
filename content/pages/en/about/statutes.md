The Paris Institute for Advanced Study follows the model of the Institutes for Advanced Study, pioneered in particular by the Institute for Advanced Study founded in Princeton in 1930, which has hosted leading figures such as Albert Einstein, Kurt Gödel and Clifford Geertz.

Established in 2008 on the initiative of the Fondation Maison des sciences de l'homme, in collaboration with the École des hautes études en sciences sociales and the École normale supérieure, the Institute became an independent body in 2011.

The IEA de Paris is an association governed by the 1901 Act, established on 1 January 2011.
