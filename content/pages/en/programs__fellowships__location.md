Whether they are staying for ten months or just a few weeks, researchers in residence at the IEA in Paris enjoy excellent facilities and a great deal of freedom in their work.

On arrival, residents are provided with a workspace within the Hôtel de Lauzun, including a fully equipped office (computer, internet access, printing facilities) and access to meeting rooms. They also have the option of having lunch on site, with a contribution towards the cost.

Researchers are accommodated at the Fondation Victor Lyon, within the Cité internationale universitaire de Paris. The Cité internationale universitaire is located in the 14th arrondissement of Paris, near the Charléty Stadium and Montsouris Park. Every year, it welcomes over 10,000 students, researchers and artists from all over the world, who live in one of the Cité’s forty houses.

The Cité internationale universitaire de Paris is a vast campus featuring extensive green spaces. It offers a wide range of sporting, musical and cultural activities open to all residents, as well as social events designed to encourage interaction and exchange. A bank, medical services and libraries are also available on campus.
