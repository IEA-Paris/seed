title: Publications

The Institute enables its residents to publish the results of their research carried out at the IEA in Paris in the form of journal articles or scientific books.

Since 2022, the IEA in Paris has launched the Proceedings of the Paris Institute for Advanced Study (PPIAS), an open-source digital platform designed to make the publications of researchers affiliated with the Institute accessible. Assigned an ISSN, this online journal enables the publication of numerous open-access contributions in the form of citable articles, complete with a DOI, text and multimedia files (video, audio). The wide range of content arising from conferences, series of meetings, seminars and symposia organised at the Institute, as well as researchers’ working papers on various topics across multiple disciplines, is brought together on this platform for sharing knowledge produced at the IEA in Paris. Discover [PPIAS](https://paris.pias.science/)
